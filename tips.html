Cyber Safety Tips for Farmers

Essential guidelines to protect yourself from cyber threats

Essential Safety Tips

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords with 12+ characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers & symbols. Avoid using personal information like birth dates or names. Use our Password Generator for secure passwords.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Always enable 2FA on your banking apps, email, and social media accounts. This adds an extra layer of security even if your password is compromised.

3. Avoid Suspicious Links

Never click on links in unsolicited emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages. Always verify the sender and type URLs directly into your browser.

4. Protect Personal Information

Never share personal details like Aadhaar number, bank account details, OTP, or passwords over phone calls, emails, or messages. Government agencies never ask for such information.

5. Keep Software Updated

Regularly update your phone, computer, and apps. Security updates fix vulnerabilities that hackers can exploit.

6. Use Secure Wi-Fi

Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking or sensitive transactions. Use secure, password-protected networks at home.

7. Install Antivirus Software

Use reputable antivirus software on all devices and keep it updated. Scan files before opening them.

8. Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter cyber crime, report immediately to cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • 🚨 Unsolicited calls claiming to be from bank/government
  • 🚨 Messages asking for OTP, PIN, or personal details
  • 🚨 Too-good-to-be-true offers (loans, prizes, jobs)
  • 🚨 Urgent threats or deadlines to act immediately
  • 🚨 Spelling mistakes or poor grammar in official messages
  • 🚨 Requests to download unknown apps or software
