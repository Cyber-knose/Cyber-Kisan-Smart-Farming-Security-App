CRITICAL
Wheat Rust
Puccinia triticina
Wheat Barley
Symptoms
  • Orange-brown pustules on leaves
  • Yellowing of leaf tissue
  • Premature leaf death
  • Reduced grain filling
Treatment Options
Propiconazole Tebuconazole Azoxystrobin
Prevention Tips
  • Use resistant varieties
  • Practice crop rotation
  • Monitor weather conditions
  • Apply preventive fungicides
MODERATE
Rice Blast
Magnaporthe oryzae
Rice Wheat
Symptoms
  • Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves
  • Grey centers with dark borders
  • Neck rot in severe cases
  • Reduced yield
Treatment Options
Tricyclazole Isoprothiolane Propiconazole
Prevention Tips
  • Avoid excessive nitrogen
  • Maintain proper spacing
  • Monitor humidity levels
  • Use resistant varieties
LOW
Cotton Bollworm
Helicoverpa armigera
Cotton Tomato Chickpea
Symptoms
  • Holes in flowers and bolls
  • Frass (insect droppings) present
  • Damaged reproductive parts
  • Yield reduction
Treatment Options
Bt Cotton Nuclear polyhedrosis virus Pheromone traps
Prevention Tips
  • Use Bt cotton varieties
  • Install pheromone traps
  • Monitor crop regularly
  • Practice crop rotation
