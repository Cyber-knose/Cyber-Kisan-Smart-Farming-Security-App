Common Cyber Crimes Targeting Farmers

Many cyber crimes involve third parties like scammers or hackers. Here are key categories:

1. Phishing/Scams (Fake Offers)

Scammers send fake messages pretending to be banks or agencies, tricking you into sharing info or money. Example: Fake loan offers for farming equipment.

2. Data Theft (Identity Stealing)

Hackers steal data from apps/websites to impersonate you. Example: Using Aadhaar details to access subsidies illegally.

3. Malware/Ransomware (Harmful Software)

Viruses lock devices until ransom is paid. Example: Infecting farm apps to demand payment.

4. QR Code Fraud

Fake QR codes lead to sites that steal card details or install malware.

5. Deepfake Crime

AI-altered media for fraud or harassment. Example: Voice cloning for unauthorized transactions.
