Helpful Resources

Trusted websites and tools for cyber safety and farming support

Government & Official Resources

These are trusted government websites and portals for farmers and cyber safety:

Agriculture Ministry

URL: agriwelfare.gov.in

Official website of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India. Provides information on schemes, subsidies, and welfare programs for farmers, including digital tools for accessing benefits securely.

Cyber Crime Portal

URL: cybercrime.gov.in

The official portal for reporting cyber crimes in India. File complaints online about phishing, hacking, or other digital threats, and get guidance on legal actions.

Farmer Helpline

URL: krishakjagat.org

A platform offering helpline services, news, and resources for farmers. Includes advice on agriculture, market prices, and can help connect you to support for cyber-related issues affecting farming.

CERT-IN

URL: cert-in.org.in

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team, providing alerts, advisories, and incident response for cyber security threats in India. Report incidents and get expert help.

Cyber Security Tools

Check if Hacked

URL: haveibeenpwned.com

A free service to check if your email or password has been compromised in data breaches. Enter your details to see if your information is at risk and take preventive steps.

Scan for Viruses

URL: virustotal.com

An online tool to scan files, URLs, or domains for malware. Upload suspicious files or links to detect viruses, trojans, or other threats before they harm your device.

Take It Down

URL: takeitdown.ncmec.org

A service by the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) to report and remove child sexual abuse material or exploitation content online. Useful if you encounter harmful content.

Banking & Finance

SBI Net Banking

URL: retail.sbi.bank.in

State Bank of India's official online banking portal. Use it for secure transactions, but always ensure you're on the official site to avoid phishing scams targeting bank details.
