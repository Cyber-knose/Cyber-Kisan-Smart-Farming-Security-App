About Cyber Kisan

Learn more about our mission to protect farmers from cyber crimes

Our Mission

Cyber Kisan is a revolutionary digital safety platform specifically designed for Indian farmers. Created by Priyanshu Jangra, this app provides comprehensive resources, safety tips, interactive quizzes, and AI-powered chatbot support to protect farmers' data and livelihoods in the digital age.

Key Features

  • Education Hub: Learn about common cyber threats targeting farmers
  • AI Chatbot: 24/7 support for cyber safety questions
  • Interactive Quiz: Test your knowledge and earn badges
  • Resource Library: Access trusted government portals and tools
  • Password Generator: Create strong, secure passwords
  • Direct Reporting: Quick links to report cyber crimes

Why Farmers Need Cyber Protection

With increasing digital adoption in agriculture - from online subsidy applications to digital payments - farmers have become prime targets for cyber criminals. Our app bridges the knowledge gap and provides practical tools to stay safe online.

Awards & Recognition

This platform is winner-ready with innovative features designed for real-world impact. The app combines traditional farming wisdom with modern cybersecurity practices.
